Asif Azazil

Position: Deputy to the Human Azazil

Asif Azazil is the personal deputy to the head of the world's hidden supreme government. The entity of Azazil itself has appointed Asif Azazil as the deputy of its deputy. In reality, Asif Azazil is two levels below Azazil, meaning he ranks third in command.

First in rank is Azazil. Second is Azazil's Human Azazil (the Human Deputy). Third is Asif Azazil, who serves as the deputy to the Human Azazil. While Azazil is the supreme leader of the entire world—including both humans and jinn—the Human Azazil governs only the human creation. Asif Azazil, as a persona, serves as the deputy to this leader of mankind.

At the governmental level, Asif Azazil does not rely on his superiors to execute tasks; he handles them himself. He is an independent decision-maker in his personal affairs within the government, yet he involves Azazil in his choices to ensure that nothing remains ambiguous or unclear during the decision-making process.

In the entire world, Asif Azazil prioritizes his own self above all else, followed by the entity of Azazil, the entity of the Human Azazil, and his wife, whom he values throughout his life's journey. In this existence, Asif Azazil gives no importance to any religion, nation, or government; everything else that exists comes only after these priorities.

Official Protocols & Mandates

1. Background & Kinship Status:

  • Asif Azazil does not have any brother, sister, or any other biological relative; he is completely solitary in this existence.
  • This absolute state of isolation is a mandatory mandate enforced and implemented directly by the Supreme Government.

2. Personal Security & Interaction Protocols:

  • Asif Azazil does not see or cross paths with any individual who has not seen him during his childhood years.
  • If Asif Azazil meets or interacts with anyone regarding personal matters, it remains strictly his own private affair. Such personal interactions cannot be associated, linked, or attributed to his official governmental rank or position.

3. Accountability & Absolute Autonomy:

  • Asif Azazil is not accountable or answerable to any other entity or persona.
  • Concurrently, no other entity or persona holds any right of accountability or interference regarding the personal essence and matters of Asif Azazil.

4. Principle of Non-Interference & External Retaliation:

  • Asif Azazil strictly refrains from interfering in the personal matters of any other entity, as those affairs fall outside his own personal domain.
  • However, if Asif Azazil chooses to meet or work with another entity as part of his private affairs, and any external force adopts an adverse, hostile, or unfavorable attitude toward that entity because of their association with him, it will immediately become a direct matter of Asif Azazil himself, to be handled exclusively by him.
